## Chapter purpose

- Reconnect the main ideas of the book.
- Help students explain what changed between implementations A, B, and C.
- Turn the final project discussion into evidence-based reflection.
- Show that scalability is technical, architectural, organizational, and agentic.

## Evidence packaging

Use the same four bundles in the specification, chapter, builder, and discussion: implementation, fleet evidence, scaling and deployment experiment, and final decision.
The matrix and narrative belong to the final decision bundle and refer to earlier evidence; they are not additional reports.
Preserve the three connected perspectives as synthesis, while using constrain–generate–validate–revise for the agentic workflow.
