## 5 ECTS scope guidance moved from reader content

The appendices are reference material rather than a second required reading sequence.
An appendix becomes assigned reading only when the instructor names it for a lecture or project activity; otherwise, open it when its question matches a decision or failure being investigated.

The case studies follow the same rule: they are selected extension readings rather than a required cover-to-cover sequence.
Read the case assigned for an activity or the one that best matches a project question being investigated.
