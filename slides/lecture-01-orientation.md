# How does self- and peer-assessment work?

**Your work.** Link one decision you challenged, tested or reviewed.
What happened?

**Your teammates.** Give each person specific feedback on something you saw them do.

Submit your assessment in your private portfolio each phase.
Your instructor uses it and the project evidence to assign your individual score.
