# Elements of Scalable Web Applications

In this brief book, I discuss the elements behind scalable web applications.
The book contains three projects for you to build that each illustrate a facet of scalability.
By completing this book, you should understand different ways to build web applications while appreciating the problems related to scaling them in particular.
It is not only infrastructure that we have to consider but also the way we build them.
Therefore scalability is not only a technical concern but also a social one as it has to do with the way we work.

By its nature, the book is more on the academic side and I have included several references to each chapter to read to further your understanding.
I have tried to keep each chapter to the point and I expect you will study these additional resources to gain a fuller view of the topic.
You can consider reading this book and implementing the book projects a journey that helps to motivate different concepts discussed while deepening your understanding.

The main reason why this book exists is to capture my understanding of how to develop scalable web applications.
While the orientation is on the web, several of the principles discussed in the book go beyond the scope so even if you are not a web practitioner, you can still likely gain something by reading the book.
Now with the age of artificial intelligence (AI) driven development understanding the concepts behind scalability is perhaps more important than other so I focus more on the conceptual level than exact technical details as those tend to change over time.

## Structure of the book

I have structured the book per chapter as follows:

1. What is scalalability? -- In other words, how do we define scalability in the first place?
2. How to compose web applications? -- What are the common composition models used for web applications, how to handle with coupling and cohesion, how to keep track of architectural decisions?
3. What is the architecture of the web? -- How was the web built and what kind of constraints does that give to our designs? How to design good APIs?
4. What happens when systems face reality? -- What kind of problems might we encounter as our systems face reality and real users? How to scale beyond my own machine?
5. How to benchmark web applications? -- Why is benchmarking important? How to benchmark effectively?
6. How to evolve web applications? -- How to evolve API contracts over time? How to consider evolvability in our designs?
7. How to scale web applications? -- What to consider when scaling our web applications? What kind of scaling techniques exist at a technical level? How does latency caused by physical constraints affect our designs?
8. What is observability? -- What is observability and how does that contrast with monitoring? How to consider security in our designs? How to apply isolation in design?
9. Why architectural decisions are easy to make only in hindsight? -- What did we learn exactly? Why do architectural constraints become apparent only in hindsight? What kind of additional constraints (ethical, sustainability) might we have to consider?
