# ADR-002: Separate course administration workflows

**Status:** Accepted

**Date:** 2026-10-05

## Context

The current **Course setup** form combines roster import, staff, groups for every phase, deadlines, and audit assignments.
Enrollment, group formation, and phase preparation happen at different times.
A large shared form makes routine changes harder to understand and risks changing unrelated configuration.

## Decision

Provide four focused workflows under course administration, with independent saves:

| Workflow             | Owns                                                                                                   |
| -------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| Students             | Roster import and preview, manual additions, corrections, sign-in/access status, and late enrollment.  |
| Groups               | Student proposals, confirmations, unassigned students, instructor allocation, and membership approval. |
| Phase administration | The selected phase's implementation, audit, and evidence deadlines and audit assignments.              |
| Course settings      | Course identity/name, staff permissions, and the run's published rubric selection.                     |

Keep review and grading as their existing dedicated workflows.
Link unresolved administrative tasks from the review queue, including unassigned students and missing audit arrangements.
Roster creation, first sign-in, and group allocation are separate events.
Group identifiers are generated by the group workflow rather than entered as part of CSV enrollment.

Preserve API authorization, revision checks, and reason/history records behind the smaller forms.
Independent saves must not overwrite concurrent changes to another workflow.

## Trigger

The instructor found Course setup overloaded and needed to enroll students before deciding groups or requiring account activation.

## Consequences

**Positive:**

- The instructor can complete a clear task without editing every phase.
- Late enrollment and later group formation fit the normal interface.
- Students and staff share a visible account of unresolved administrative work.

**Negative:**

- Navigation and API update boundaries must be redesigned together.
- Smaller forms still need consistent validation of cross-workflow constraints.

**Neutral:**

- Course staff authorization remains separate from book feedback moderation.
- Rubric changes must retain the version used by historical runs and assessments.

## Alternatives considered

### Retain one form with more explanations

Better labels would help, but would not separate different timing, ownership, or save operations.

### Move all administration to external spreadsheets

This would split group, deadline, access, and grading state across systems and make the student view harder to explain.

## Implementation and verification

The current combined setup form remains implemented; the split is pending.
Verify that an instructor can import students, form groups later, and edit one phase independently without losing another saved change.
Verify student access cannot invoke any staff operation.
Update [course-system.md](../../course-system.md) when the new workflows exist.
