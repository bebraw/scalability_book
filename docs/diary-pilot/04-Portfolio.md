## Select a lecture entry

The workspace labels entries by course lecture number, from 1 to 9.
The Obsidian draft also gives each lecture’s position within its phase.
Submit an idea or a connection for seven different lectures across your three portfolios.
For participation points, identify your live participation or announced remote equivalent and its evidence.
Choose one concrete idea, failure, model or trade-off from the entry.
Which observation, earlier assumption or decision gives that idea meaning in your project?

You can paste an excerpt into the matching field.
For audio or video, attach the chosen file and describe its relevance.
Use source links that staff can open outside your vault.

## Look back across the phase

- **Self-assessment.** What did you contribute, which commitments did you meet or miss, and what will you improve?
- **Feedback.** What specific feedback would you give each teammate?
  On the approved solo route, use evidence and limitations from the independent implementation review.
- **Response.** What adjustment did you make after feedback?
  Name one team or solo working condition that affected the result.
- **A decision you influenced.** Which consequential decision did you challenge, test or review?
  Link the evidence and explain the outcome.

Saving a portfolio draft or uploading a file gives course staff access to that material.
