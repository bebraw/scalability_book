# My learning diary

Obsidian calls this **Course diary** folder a _vault_.
Each lecture note is a text file you can edit. [First-time setup with screenshots](https://scalableweb.dev/diary/#start-with-the-first-lecture)

After the first lecture, open [Lecture 1](Establish/Lecture%201.md) and write your own response to the cache question on a new line below the prompt.
Obsidian saves as you type.
Next time, open **Establish → Lecture 1** in the file list to find your prediction again.

Start with something you noticed, questioned or tried.
Each lecture has a question you can use if the page feels blank.
A few sentences in your own words, or a reference to an audio or video entry, can capture the idea.

## Before and after class

Each lecture note links to its draft slides and an optional self-study activity.
For later lectures, skim the slides before class and jot down one question you want to bring.
After class, revisit your question and add one observation from a demonstration, a team presentation or your project that helped you answer it.
Reopen the same slide link to see revisions as the course progresses. [All lecture slides](https://scalableweb.dev/slides/)

## An example diary entry

> I expect a second worker to drain our queue faster.
> I’ll time how long one worker and then two take to finish the same 600 jobs.
>
> One worker took 61 seconds and two took 60. The trace showed both workers waiting for a database connection.
> Before trying a third worker, I’d measure how much time each job spent waiting for a connection.

## Open readings inside Obsidian

On desktop, you can read the book beside your notes with the built-in Web viewer.
It works in Restricted Mode.

1. Open **Settings → Core plugins** and turn on **Web viewer**.
2. In the Settings sidebar, choose **Web viewer** and turn on **Open external links**.
3. Open [Lecture 1](Establish/Lecture%201.md).
   Right-click the note’s tab and choose **Split right** to keep your diary visible on the left.
4. In the right pane, follow **Reading** in the lecture note.
   The chapter should open beside your note. [Obsidian Web viewer](https://obsidian.md/help/plugins/web-viewer)

## Keep a passage

To send a passage straight to a lecture, open **Settings → Community plugins** in this vault, exit Restricted Mode and enable **Course diary**.
Select a sentence in the book and choose **Add book highlight** at the bottom of the page.
In Obsidian, choose the lecture, check the quote and its source, then choose **Save highlight**.

In Restricted Mode, choose **Copy highlight** in the book and paste it into your lecture note.
Below the quote, add what made you pause. [Course diary guide](https://scalableweb.dev/diary/)

When a result or someone’s feedback changes your thinking, return to the original entry.
Add the date and what you would say now.
Keeping the earlier prediction gives you something concrete to compare.

At the end of a phase, [prepare your private portfolio](04-Portfolio.md) from passages you choose.
Across the course, submit an entry for at least seven of the nine lectures.
Keep this folder outside the group's project repository.
For shared specifications, decisions and test results, open your team's project repository as a second vault. [Group work in Obsidian](https://scalableweb.dev/diary/#work-with-your-project-group-in-obsidian)
The course accepts written, audio and video diaries. [Diary and participation criteria](https://scalableweb.dev/project/grading/#participation-and-learning-diary-3-points-per-lecture)

## Ask the course TA

When you want a hint or a check on your reasoning, select the part of your entry you want to discuss.
With the course plugin, right-click the selection, choose **Discuss with the course TA** and review your question.
For example, “What observation would challenge my explanation?”
Choose **Use Obsidian TA** if you want to use your OpenAI API account from this computer.
You can also open the book with an existing companion.
In the book, review the excerpt and choose **Send message** when you are ready. [TA setup and other providers](https://scalableweb.dev/diary/#discuss-a-question-with-the-ta)

Back in your diary, add what you now think or what you still want to check.
If you keep wording from the assistant, identify it as such.

To talk with classmates, select your saved initial thinking and choose **Use as my initial thinking** from the right-click menu.
Review the book passage before opening the class discussion.
You can write a separate question or reply for your group once you are there.
