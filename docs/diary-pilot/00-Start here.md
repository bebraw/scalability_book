# My learning diary

Open the phase you are studying and write under the current lecture.
Start with something you noticed, questioned or tried.
Each lecture has a question you can use if the page feels blank.
A few sentences in your own words, or a reference to an audio or video entry, can capture the idea.

| Phase                        | Course lectures | Project                           |
| ---------------------------- | --------------- | --------------------------------- |
| [Establish](01-Establish.md) | 1–3             | Build and measure A               |
| [Challenge](02-Challenge.md) | 4–6             | Build B and examine an assumption |
| [Compare](03-Compare.md)     | 7–9             | Build C and defend a decision     |

To keep a passage next to your response, select it on the book website and choose **Copy highlight**.
Paste the linked quote into the lecture entry and add what made you pause.
The optional **Course diary** plugin can place it under the right lecture for you. [Course diary guide](https://scalableweb.dev/diary/)

When a result or someone’s feedback changes your thinking, return to the original entry.
Add the date and what you would say now.
Keeping the earlier prediction gives you something concrete to compare.

At the end of a phase, [prepare your private portfolio](04-Portfolio.md) from passages you choose.
Keep this folder outside the group's project repository.
The course accepts written, audio and video diaries. [Diary and participation criteria](https://scalableweb.dev/project/grading/#participation-and-learning-diary-3-points-per-lecture)

## Ask the course TA

When you want a hint or a check on your reasoning, copy the part of your entry you want to discuss.
Open that lecture’s reading link in Chrome on the computer running your configured companion.
Choose **Teaching assistant → Discuss**, check the reading, then paste the excerpt and add your question.
For example, “What observation would challenge my explanation?” Choose **Send message** when you are ready. [Discuss a question with the TA](https://scalableweb.dev/diary/#discuss-a-question-with-the-ta)

Back in your diary, add what you now think or what you still want to check.
If you keep wording from the assistant, identify it as such.
