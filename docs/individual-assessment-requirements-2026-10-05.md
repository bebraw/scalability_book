# Mandatory individual work: course comparison and proposal

Reviewed on 5 October 2026.
The course offers 90 shared project points and 63 individual points.
Before this revision, the total-point rule allowed a student with all 90 project points and no individual points to pass with grade 1.
The draft now requires individual engagement independently of the aggregate mark.

These examples inform the proposed structure.
The Macquarie guide also provides an alternative assessment route for some students who attempted work but missed the participation requirement.
The numerical thresholds below are choices for this course's nine lectures and three phases.

## Proposed requirements using existing evidence

Retain the 153-point maximum, the ten-point minimum for each project, and the 50% total-point minimum.
Add individual requirements that can be checked from the existing private portfolios and criterion scores:

1. Complete two of the three lecture activities in each phase, for six across the course.
   A counted activity earns the participation point through live participation or the announced equivalent remote activity, and both existing diary points for a concrete idea and its connection to evidence or a decision.
2. Submit self- and peer-assessment for every phase and earn at least six of its twelve points.
   Each phase must include at least one point for reliable contribution and shared ownership, supported by the existing project record and contribution evidence.

The two-per-phase requirement keeps engagement distributed across Establish, Challenge, and Compare while permitting one missed activity per phase.
It retains the remote-equivalent path.

An approved absence or support arrangement should specify the replacement activity or adjusted individual requirement as well as any change to available points.
Publish the correction and completion arrangements before the affected phase and retain the original evidence and decision when a result changes.

## Why an aggregate individual minimum is insufficient

- A minimum of half the 63 individual points could be met by self- and peer-assessment alone, leaving lecture participation entirely absent.
- A minimum of half the 27 lecture-and-diary points could be met by diary points alone: nine entries can earn eighteen points without earning any participation points.
- A course-wide self- and peer-assessment minimum could allow one phase's assessment to be omitted.
- Six assessment points per phase could come entirely from reflection and feedback, so the existing contribution criterion also needs a nonzero result when contribution is required.

Check participation through the completed activities, assessment through phase-specific scores, and contribution through the existing criterion.
The instructor already assesses this evidence; the additional work is recording and displaying whether each minimum is met.

The student-facing scoring record should show each project's result, completed lecture activities, phase assessment scores and contribution credit, total points, and the specific unmet requirement when the course is not yet passed.
